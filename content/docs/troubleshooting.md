# Troubleshooting

- Confirm that the installed Windows edition matches the license.
- Open **Settings → System → Activation** and run the Activation troubleshooter when available.
- Check that the PC is connected to the internet for online activation.
- For organization-managed licenses, contact your IT administrator.
- For retail or OEM license issues, contact the seller, device manufacturer, or Microsoft Support.

This app reports the response from Windows; it cannot resolve license or account issues on Microsoft's activation service.
